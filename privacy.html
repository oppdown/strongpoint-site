Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This policy covers the Strongpoint game and its account service.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your Google account identifier, email address and display name. We ask Google only for the basic openid, email and profile permissions. We never see your Google password.
  • From playing: your in-game callsign, experience and level, match statistics (such as kills, deaths and matches played), your friends list, and invite codes you have redeemed.
  • Technical: the network address your game connects from, kept in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

To sign you in, decide whether your account has playtest access, keep your progress and statistics, show your friends list, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and this site uses no trackers or cookies.

Where it is kept

On servers run by the Strongpoint development team. Google processes sign-in under its own privacy policy.

How long

For as long as your account exists. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing philliphinsley@gmail.com. You can also remove Strongpoint's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Strongpoint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes, and change the date at the top.
